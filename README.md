## 🎮 Overview

Knight Fall blends the fun and frenzy of elimination-style gameplay with the power of blockchain. Players battle it out in wild physics-based obstacle courses, with one goal: **be the last Knight standing**. As rounds progress, players are eliminated until a single winner remains.

With seamless wallet login, on-chain rewards, and customizable NFT skins, Knight Fall brings the thrill of *Fall Guys* into the decentralized world—secure, fast, and rewarding.
